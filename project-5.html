Word-guessing game

FyzzGames is a word-guessing game I developed and integrated into an online store application, serving as an interactive tool for customers to earn shopping discounts. Built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—with Bootstrap for UI components and SweetAlert2 for interactive notifications—the game features 10 challenges across three difficulty levels that must be completed sequentially. Customers who successfully complete all challenges and reach a specific score can instantly claim a discount coupon via the game's built-in reward-claiming feature. In addition to scoring and clue systems, the game includes dark mode, background music, and a mobile-responsive design to enhance the combined shopping and gaming experience. I designed and built the entire game logic on the client side without a backend, utilizing `localStorage` to store player data and reward-claim status; this serves as a simple gamification strategy to boost customer engagement and retention for the online store.
